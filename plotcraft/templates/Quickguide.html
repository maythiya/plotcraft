Guide PlotCraft

An end-to-end writing workspace, from outlining and worldbuilding to drafting and export.

System overview

PlotCraft helps writers stay focused by organizing the creative process into connected sections:

1. My novels
2. Worldbuilding (story reference)
3. Scenes (scene cards and structure)
4. Timeline (chronological order)
5. Export (EPUB and PDF)
1

My novels — manuscript management

A focused space for writing and organizing chapters.

Main features

  • Hierarchical Structure: Organize work as Novel (Project) > Chapter so everything stays easy to find.
  • EPUB Export: When the manuscript is ready, export an E-Book (.epub) for publishing or reading on an e-reader.
2

Worldbuilding — build a connected universe

A reference library for your story, organized into clear categories.

Characters

Create layered characters with names, backstories, and goals.

  • Portrait
  • Goals
  • Strengths/Weaknesses
  • Traits and background

Locations

Design believable settings, environments, and societies.

  • Terrain and climate
  • Society and economy
  • History and myths
  • Culture

Items & artifacts

Track important objects, weapons, and magical artifacts.

  • Special abilities
  • Limitations and conditions
  • Origin and history
  • Appearance
3

Scenes — the heart of your story

Plan each scene without losing focus.

Scene elements

The Scenes workspace provides the key details writers need:

  • Goal What does the character want in this scene?
  • Conflict What obstacle or conflict occurs?
  • Outcome What is the outcome, and how does it lead into the next scene?

Linking

Connect worldbuilding information directly to a scene:

Link Characters Link Location Link Items POV Character
4

Timeline — timeline

Review event order and prevent timeline inconsistencies.

Create important events, arrange them in order, and connect each event to its related scene.

Technology behind PlotCraft

Backend & Database

Powered by Django Framework (Python) and MySQL for secure, structured story data.

Frontend

Built with HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and Alpine.js for a smooth and responsive interface.
